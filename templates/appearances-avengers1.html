// 2012 Appearance

The Avengers

Lead Role — Earth's Mightiest Heroes

← Back to Movies

Tony joins Earth's newly assembled defenders to stop Loki's planetary invasion. Working alongside Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye, Tony carries a nuclear warhead through the wormhole into deep space to destroy the Chitauri command ship, nearly sacrificing himself in the process.

Mark VI Mark VII

Iron Man Mark VI Flying

Tony suits up in the Mark VI, soaring through the sky to assist in Loki's capture in Stuttgart, Germany.

Stark Tower Arc Reactor

The clean, self-sustaining Arc Reactor designed by Tony Stark to fully power the newly constructed Stark Tower in New York.

Welcome to Stark Tower

Tony returns to Stark Tower, welcoming Pepper Potts after successfully taking the tower completely off the city power grid.

Confronting Loki

Tony Stark meets with Loki in the Stark Tower penthouse, warning him that his planetary invasion plan will fail against the Avengers.

Studying Tesseract Energy

Tony Stark uses S.H.I.E.L.D. laboratory interfaces to study the Tesseract's gamma energy signatures, seeking to pinpoint its location.

Steve & Tony Forest Stand-Off

Tony Stark and Steve Rogers in the forest of East Europe, confronting Thor who has taken Loki from S.H.I.E.L.D. custody.

Iron Man vs. Thor

Tony Stark in the Mark VI clashes with Thor in the woods, testing the limits of both Asgardian strength and Stark technology.

Lightning Charge 400%

Thor strikes Iron Man with a massive bolt of lightning, which unexpectedly charges Tony's suit capacity to an extreme 400%.

Steve Intervenes

Steve Rogers steps between Iron Man and Thor, using his vibranium shield to stop their destructive woodland brawl.

Helicarrier Bridge Meeting

Tony Stark meets Steve Rogers and Nick Fury on the Helicarrier bridge, injecting some of his signature sarcasm into S.H.I.E.L.D. protocol.

Science Bros Bonding

Tony and Bruce Banner work together in the laboratory, immediately bonding over their brilliant scientific minds and mutual respect.

Hacking S.H.I.E.L.D.

Tony Stark uses a custom device to hack into S.H.I.E.L.D.'s central database to discover what Fury is hiding under "Phase 2".

"Put the Suit On"

Steve Rogers confronts Tony Stark in the lab, telling him: "You're not the guy to make the sacrifice play. Put the suit on, let's go a few rounds."

Repairing the Helicarrier Engine

When Loki's agents damage the Helicarrier, Tony suits up and uses his high-powered red lasers to cut away debris blocking the engine rotor.

Pushing the Helicarrier Turbine

With the turbine stopped, Tony must physically push the massive rotor blades inside the engine chamber to restart flight propulsion.

Confronting Dr. Selvig

Tony arrives back in New York at Stark Tower, speaking to the mind-controlled Dr. Selvig to try and shut down the Tesseract portal machine.

Mark VI in New York

Tony flies down to Stark Tower in his heavily battle-damaged Mark VI armor to confront Loki face-to-face inside the penthouse.

"Performance Issues"

Tony Stark pours himself a drink while talking to Loki. Loki's attempt to use the scepter fails against Tony's metal chest reactor, prompting a classic Stark quip.

Thrown off the Penthouse

An angered Loki throws Tony off the Stark Tower penthouse, sending him plummeting hundreds of feet to the streets below.

Mark VII Mid-Air Assembled

Launched as a pod by J.A.R.V.I.S., the advanced Mark VII armor homes in on Tony's wrist scanners and fully assembles around him mid-fall.

Loki vs. Iron Man

Tony returns to the penthouse in the Mark VII, immediately firing a repulsor blast that knocks Loki down: "And there's one other guy..."

Battle of New York Assembles

The original Avengers unite on the battlefield, standing in a protective circle as the Chitauri leviathans swarm the city.

Mark VII Flying Fire

Tony Stark flies through the canyons of Manhattan, using his repulsors and lasers to distract and defeat waves of alien invaders.

Intercepting the Nuclear Missile

The World Security Council launches a nuclear warhead at New York. Knowing the risk, Tony intercepts the missile, grabbing it mid-flight.

Through the Portal

Tony guides the nuclear warhead up into the spatial portal, bringing it directly into deep space towards the Chitauri command ship.

A One Way Trip

Tony looks at Pepper's incoming call as his systems power down in deep space. He releases the missile, accepting his sacrifice as the command ship explodes.

Falling Unpowered

Tony Stark slips back through the portal just as it closes, falling unpowered and unconscious towards the streets of Manhattan.

Hulk Catches Tony

Hulk catches Tony mid-air, sliding down a building to cushion the fall, while Thor immediately rushes over to check on him.

Tony Stark Wakes Up

Hulk's roar startles Tony awake. Exhausted, Tony quips: "What just happened? Please tell me nobody kissed me. We won?"

Confronting Loki at Stark Tower

The team surrounds Loki in the Stark Tower penthouse, with Hawkeye aiming an arrow at him while Loki surrenders: "If it's all the same to you, I'll have that drink now."

Central Park Departure

The Avengers gather at Central Park to say goodbye as Thor uses the Tesseract to return Loki and the artifact to Asgard.

Post-Credits Shawarma

The exhausted Avengers sit together in a heavily damaged local restaurant, eating shawarma in complete, quiet exhaustion.
