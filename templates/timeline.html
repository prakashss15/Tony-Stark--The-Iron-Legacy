THE STARK TIMELINE

From Prodigy to Legend

1970

Born a Stark

Anthony Edward Stark is born to Howard and Maria Stark. Son of the legendary weapons developer and co-founder of S.H.I.E.L.D.

Origin
1984

Builds First Circuit Board

At age 4, Tony builds his first circuit board. By age 6, he builds his first engine. A prodigy who would one day redefine technology itself.

Genius
1988

MIT at 17

Tony graduates summa cum laude from MIT with dual degrees in physics and engineering. The youngest graduate in the school's history.

Education
1991

Loss of Parents

Howard and Maria Stark are killed in what appears to be a car accident. Tony, now 21, inherits Stark Industries and becomes the world's youngest CEO of a Fortune 500 company.

Tragedy
2008

Captured in Afghanistan

During a weapons demonstration, Tony is captured by the Ten Rings terrorist group. Gravely injured by his own Jericho missile, he builds the Mark I suit in a cave with a box of scraps — and escapes.

Iron Man
2008

"I Am Iron Man"

Tony reveals his identity to the world, breaking the superhero convention of secret identities. Builds the iconic Mark III suit and defeats Obadiah Stane (Iron Monger). Stark Industries pivots from weapons to clean energy.

Revelation
2010

Palladium Poisoning & New Element

The Arc Reactor's palladium core is slowly killing Tony. With help from his father's hidden research, he creates a new element to power the reactor. Battles Ivan Vanko (Whiplash) and meets Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff).

Iron Man 2
2012

The Battle of New York

Tony joins the newly formed Avengers to fight Loki's Chitauri invasion. He carries a nuclear missile through a portal into space, nearly sacrificing himself to save Manhattan. This event haunts him with PTSD.

Avengers
2013

The Mandarin & Iron Legion

Haunted by anxiety after New York, Tony obsessively builds the Iron Legion — 35 suits, each with unique capabilities. Faces the Mandarin and Aldrich Killian, and destroys all his suits in the "Clean Slate" protocol.

Iron Man 3
2015

Ultron & The Vision

Tony creates the Ultron AI as a peacekeeping program, which goes rogue and threatens humanity. Deploys the Hulkbuster to fight a mind-controlled Hulk. Helps create the Vision from J.A.R.V.I.S. to stop Ultron.

Age of Ultron
2016

Civil War

The Avengers split over the Sokovia Accords. Tony supports government oversight while Steve Rogers opposes it. Tony discovers the Winter Soldier killed his parents, leading to a devastating fight with Captain America.

Civil War
2017

Mentoring Spider-Man

Tony becomes a mentor to Peter Parker, providing him with advanced technology. Though he keeps Peter at arm's length, Tony becomes a father figure — a role he never expected but grew to cherish.

Homecoming
2018

The Battle on Titan

Tony deploys the nano-tech Mark L suit against Thanos on Titan. Despite everything, Thanos completes the Infinity Gauntlet and snaps half of all life away. Tony is stranded in space, near death.

Infinity War
2023

The Time Heist

After 5 years living a quiet life with Pepper and their daughter Morgan, Tony cracks time travel in a single night. He leads the Avengers on a Time Heist through the Quantum Realm to collect the Infinity Stones from the past.

Endgame
2023

The Ultimate Sacrifice

In the final battle against Thanos, Tony seizes the Infinity Stones from the gauntlet and performs the Snap — wiping out Thanos and his army. The power of all six stones kills Tony. His final words: "I am Iron Man."

I Am Iron Man
∞

The Legacy Lives On

Tony Stark's sacrifice saved trillions of lives across the universe. His legacy endures through Stark Industries, the technology he created, the heroes he inspired, and the daughter who carries his name. "I love you 3000."

Forever
STARK.

A tribute to the genius, billionaire, playboy, philanthropist who saved the universe.
